1 Introduction & Overview

Welcome to StoreQuest, a single-player store management and supermarket simulation game created by developer Damir Žiher ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy informs you about how StoreQuest operates regarding information when you install and play the game on your Android device.

Key Commitment: StoreQuest is designed with a "privacy-by-design" principle. The game runs entirely offline on your device and does not collect or transmit any user data.

2 Information We Collect (None)

StoreQuest does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • Account credentials, passwords, or authentication tokens.
  • Financial, credit card, or payment transaction information.
  • Precise or approximate geographic location.
  • Device identifiers (such as IMEI, Android ID, MAC address, Advertising ID / GAID).
  • Usage statistics, browsing history, or in-game behavior analytics.

3 Local Storage & Save Data

StoreQuest saves game progress strictly locally on your device in the application's isolated sandbox storage directory (user://savegame.json and user://settings.json). This local data includes:

  • Gameplay state: Current day, store cash balance, store level, customer happiness, and quest status.
  • Store inventory: Stock counts, display shelf assignments, and product pricing.
  • Upgrades: Purchased shop expansions, equipment, and shelves.
  • Preferences: Sound effects (SFX) volume, background music volume, and selected language (Croatian, English, German, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Turkish, Italian, Polish).

This save data never leaves your device and is never synchronized to or stored on any external server or cloud service.

4 Third-Party Services & Advertising

StoreQuest does not integrate or communicate with any third-party services:

  • No Advertisements: The game does not display ads and contains no ad networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads).
  • No Analytics SDKs: No analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics for Firebase, GameAnalytics, Flurry) are embedded.
  • No Social Media Plugins: There are no social network SDKs or sharing widgets.

5 Device Permissions

StoreQuest does not require or request any sensitive runtime permissions. It does not ask for access to:

  • Camera or Microphone
  • GPS Location (Coarse or Fine)
  • Contacts, Phone State, or SMS
  • External Storage, Photos, or Media Libraries

The game requires only standard Android system features needed for standard graphic rendering and audio playback.

6 Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

StoreQuest is a fun, non-violent, family-friendly simulation game intended for audiences of all ages (PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone).

We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation regarding children (GDPR-K), and Google Play's Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect or request personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • Because the game collects no personal data whatsoever from any player, children's data is never collected, stored, or distributed.

7 Data Retention & Deletion

Because all save data is stored solely on your device, you have complete control over it. You can permanently delete your data at any time by:

  • Using the "🗑️ Delete Save / Obriši Save" button directly inside the game's Settings menu.
  • Clearing application storage in Android: Settings → Apps → StoreQuest → Storage → Clear Data / Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling the game from your device, which automatically removes all associated files.

8 International Regulations (GDPR & CCPA)

StoreQuest complies with global privacy regulations, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA).

Since we do not collect, process, or sell any personal data, there is no personal data subject to disclosure, rectification, portability, or sale.

9 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Dobrodošli u igru StoreQuest, simulaciju upravljanja trgovinom i supermarketom koju je razvio Damir Žiher ("mi", "nas" ili "naš").

Iznimno cijenimo vašu privatnost i predani smo njenoj zaštiti. Ova Pravila privatnosti objašnjavaju način na koji StoreQuest postupa s informacijama prilikom instalacije i igranja igre na vašem Android uređaju.

Ključno obećanje: StoreQuest je izrađen prema načelu "privatnosti po dizajnu" (privacy-by-design). Igra radi 100% izvanmrežno (offline) i ne prikuplja niti prenosi nikakve podatke o korisnicima.

2 Informacije koje prikupljamo (Nema prikupljanja)

StoreQuest NE prikuplja, ne pohranjuje, ne prenosi i ne dijeli nikakve osobne podatke niti podatke koji mogu identificirati pojedinca. Konkretno, NE prikupljamo:

  • Imena, e-mail adrese, telefonske brojeve ili adrese stanovanja.
  • Korisničke račune, lozinke ili autentifikacijske tokene.
  • Financijske podatke, podatke o kreditnim karticama ili plaćanjima.
  • Geografsku lokaciju (ni preciznu ni približnu).

3 Lokalna pohrana i spremanje igre

StoreQuest sprema napredak u igri isključivo lokalno na vašem uređaju unutar izoliranog direktorija aplikacije (user://savegame.json i user://settings.json). Ovi lokalni podaci uključuju:

  • Stanje igre: Trenutni radni dan, stanje novca u blagajni, razinu trgovine, zadovoljstvo kupaca i zadatke.
  • Zalihe trgovine: Količinu robe u skladištu, raspored artikala na policama i postavljene prodajne cijene.
  • Nadogradnje: Kupljena proširenja trgovine, opremu i police.
  • Postavke: Glasnoću zvučnih efekata (SFX), glasnoću glazbe i odabrani jezik sučelja (hrvatski, engleski, njemački, španjolski, portugalski, francuski, turski, talijanski, poljski).

Ovi podaci nikada ne napuštaju vaš uređaj i nikada se ne sinkroniziraju s vanjskim poslužiteljima ili uslugama u oblaku.
